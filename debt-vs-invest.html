💡 How to use this tool (Click to collapse)

Not sure whether to pay off debt faster or keep investing? This tool compares three common strategies to help you decide what makes the most mathematical sense for your situation.

  • Strategy A (Status Quo): Keep paying your loan and investing exactly as you are now.
  • Strategy B (Accelerate): Make extra payments on your loan while maintaining your current investment contributions.
  • Strategy C (Prioritise Debt): Temporarily pause your investments and redirect that money to your loan. Once the debt is cleared, invest that entire freed-up monthly cash flow.

Tip: Start by entering your basic loan and investment balances below. If you know your specific fees or insurance premiums, click "Show advanced options" inside each card.

📚 Methodology, Formulas & South African References

Amortisation Engine

  • Monthly rate r = annual_rate / 12
  • Interestm = opening_balance × r
  • If instalment < interest, negative amortisation is flagged (loan never settles at that payment level).
  • Final payment is truncated so balance never goes negative; no interest or fees are charged after settlement.

Key Caveats

  • Projections are not guarantees. Investment returns are assumed constant; real markets are volatile.
  • Early settlement quotations from your lender always override this model.
  • National Credit Act 34 of 2005: Reg.43–45 set maximum initiation and monthly service fees. The commonly cited monthly service fee cap for unsecured credit is R69 including VAT.
Lender-specific premium formulas and early-settlement quotation conventions vary. Always confirm with your credit provider and a registered FSP before acting.
