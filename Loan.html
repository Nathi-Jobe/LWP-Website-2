Formulas & Assumptions

Amortisation engine

For each month m:

  • Monthly rate r = annual_rate / 12
  • Interestm = opening_balance × r
  • Closing balance = opening + interest + capitalised fees − principal repaid
  • If instalment < interest, negative amortisation is flagged (loan never settles at that payment level).
  • Final payment is truncated so balance never goes negative; no interest or fees are charged after settlement.
  • Credit-life premium follows the user-selected rule: fixed, verified declining (proportional to balance), or unknown (held constant but flagged unverified).
  • Admin fee is charged only for months in which the loan is outstanding at month-start.
Investment growth

Monthly net rate i = (annual_return − annual_fees − annual_tax) / 12 applied to opening balance; contribution added at start or end of month per user selection.

Compound formula: FV = PV·(1+i)ⁿ + C·[((1+i)ⁿ − 1)/i] (end-of-month) or with an extra (1+i) factor for start-of-month.

Net financial position

Net = Investment assets + Emergency reserve − Outstanding loan balance. Excludes property, vehicles, retirement annuities (illiquid), and any liabilities not entered.

Strategy cash flows
  • A: scheduled instalment (+ any user-entered extra repayment) + normal investment contribution every month.
  • B: same as A but extra repayment is always applied (A uses it only if entered).
  • C: investment contribution + extra repayment redirected to loan until settlement; afterwards the combined freed cash flow is invested monthly.
Key caveats
  • Loan interest rate ≠ investment return: they differ in tax treatment, fees, liquidity, risk and compounding conventions.
  • Projections are not guarantees. Investment returns are assumed constant; real markets are volatile.
  • Early settlement quotations from the lender always override this model.
  • Credit-life cover ceases on settlement (subject to policy terms); no refund of past premiums is assumed.

South African References

  • National Credit Act 34 of 2005 — particularly s.105 (interest and fees), s.106 (credit life insurance) and the general affordability / disclosure framework.
  • Credit Life Insurance Rules, 2017 (published under the NCA) — governs permissible credit-life cover types, maximum cover amounts, premium limits and the requirement that cover cease on full settlement (subject to policy terms).
  • National Credit Regulations — Reg.43–45 set maximum initiation and monthly service fees. The commonly cited monthly service fee cap for unsecured credit is R69 including VAT (15%); this is a cap, not a universal charge, and differs by credit category.
  • Income Tax Act — interest exemptions (s.10(1)(i)), retirement fund tax treatment, TFSA exemptions, endowment tax regimes. Tax assumptions in this tool are simplifications.
Lender-specific premium formulas, early-settlement quotation conventions and fee schedules vary. Always confirm with your credit provider and a registered financial services provider before acting.
